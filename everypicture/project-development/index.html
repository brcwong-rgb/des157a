Every Picture Project Development

Improvements and Why

The design changes I made are geared toward improving the visual look and feel. They highlight the content more clearly and create a stronger sense of hierarchy. I added clear structure through the use of H1 and H2 headings, while making the images and the carousel the main focal points. I also included an extra car image into the carousel to ensure that there is enough variety within the design.

These changes help guide the viewer’s attention more effectively while making the content more engaging for the user. I believe that by establishing a clear hierarchy in the typography it makes the content more understandable and identifiable. Also, by adding more images to the composition it adds variety and makes the user want to continue to look through all the images before leaving
